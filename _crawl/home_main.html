Teaching & Speaking

For over 20 years, I've been an arts educator in schools, libraries, and other community spaces. Using my books as a gateway, I help kids experience how fun it can be to write their own stories and draw original worlds. I also love helping adults to experience the joy of drawing, writing, and creative play.
